# Solara Moto Gear - Corporate Profile & Persona Guide

This skill serves as the foundational reference and memory for **Solara Moto Gear**, the fictional company powering the solaramoto.com ecosystem. 

Always load or consult this profile when generating copy, catalog items, dealer announcements, or customer personas to ensure perfect alignment with Solara's business model, flagship offerings, and brand positioning.

---

## 1. Company Overview & Core Products
**Solara Moto Gear** designs, manufactures, and distributes premium luggage systems, helmets, and technical motorcycle and scooter accessories. 
*   **Model Comparison**: Heavily modeled after Givi’s real-world catalog and engineering philosophy ([givi.es](https://www.givi.es)).
*   **Product Categories**:
    *   **Luggage Systems**: Hard top cases, side panniers, expandable tank bags, and soft tail bags.
    *   **Wind Protection**: High-stability windshields, aerodynamic spoilers, and handguards.
    *   **Protective Gear**: Stainless steel engine crash bars, radiator guards, and skid plates.
    *   **Technical Accessories**: Specialized, vibration-dampening smartphone/GPS mounts (including waterproof wireless charging models).

### Flagship Product
*   **The TR500 TrailMaster**: Solara's premium aluminum pannier & top-case system. It represents the pinnacle of adventure-touring durability, featuring high-grade alloy panels, dual-sealing silicone waterproof gaskets, and a robust quick-release subframe mounting system.

## 2. Dual Go-To-Market Strategy
Solara operates a hybrid B2C and B2B commercial flow, enabling it to capture direct consumer growth while expanding wholesale reach through distributors:

### A. B2C — New Business Acquisition (NBA)
*   **Goal**: Acquiring first-time riders as direct customers on [solaramoto.com](https://solaramoto.com).
*   **Flows**: Driving e-commerce sales, digital catalog interactions, and direct technical accessory support.
*   **Positioning**: Focusing on styling, simplicity of installation, and modern rider lifestyles.

### B. B2B — Existing Business Expansion (EBE)
*   **Goal**: Growing wallet share and order volumes with dealers/distributors who already stock Solara's flagship case systems.
*   **Flows**: Managed through the **Solara Dealer Portal**, involving bulk price lists, volume rebate campaigns, quarterly performance audits, and dealer incentives.
*   **Positioning**: Emphasizing high stock turnover, technical reliability, OEM compatibility, and profitable dealer margins.

## 3. Rider Persona & Attitude Segments
Solara's consumer segments mirror Givi’s real-world "attitude" design targets. When writing marketing copy or defining user categories, align them to these specific riding styles:

1.  **Urban (Commuter)**: Focused on daily city navigation, scooter top cases, smartphone mounts, compact windshields, and ease of parking.
2.  **Touring (Road)**: Prioritizes comfort over long road distances. Relies on large expandable cases, touring screens, high-capacity tank bags, and high comfort.
3.  **Adventure (Dual-Sport / Off-Road)**: Hardcore touring across rugged terrains. Uses aluminum TR500 TrailMaster cases, heavy-duty engine protections, skid plates, and direct-mount GPS navigators.
4.  **Sport**: Minimalist, aerodynamic, and high-performance. Prefers low-profile soft tail bags, carbon-finish accents, and high-speed stability.
5.  **Café Racer (Classic)**: Vintage, retro aesthetics. Uses premium leather luggage, round classic windscreens, and polished alloy mounting fixtures.
6.  **Electric**: Next-gen micro-mobility and electric commuting. Prioritizes ultra-lightweight materials, aerodynamic efficiency, and integrated USB/power connections.

## 4. Key Marketing Campaign Personas

These standard customer personas are mapped to Solara’s marketing campaigns to drive direct B2C sales and wholesale B2B dealer stock replenishment. Each campaign persona maps directly to a formal taxonomy category under Liferay’s **`L_CMP_PERSONAS`** (Personas) vocabulary:

### A. First-Time Riders
*   **Liferay Taxonomy Category**: `"First-Time Riders"`
*   **Attitude Segment**: Urban (Commuter) / Electric
*   **Targeting Priority**: **High (Direct-to-Consumer NBA)**
*   **Profile**: New, younger, or commuter riders acquiring their very first motorbike or scooter. They look for entry-level price points, easy-to-install accessories, and high-safety gear.
*   **Commercial Alignment**: B2C New Business Acquisition (NBA) on solaramoto.com.

### B. Experienced Tourers
*   **Liferay Taxonomy Category**: `"Experienced Tourers"`
*   **Attitude Segment**: Touring (Road)
*   **Targeting Priority**: **High (Core Revenue Driver)**
*   **Profile**: Seasoned highway and road-touring riders. They value long-range riding comfort, high-capacity road cases, expandable tank bags, and aerodynamically stable touring windscreens.
*   **Commercial Alignment**: Drives premium wholesale (B2B) replenishment and secondary direct B2C upgrades.

### C. The Adventure Purist
*   **Liferay Taxonomy Category**: `"Off-Road Explorer"`
*   **Attitude Segment**: Adventure (Dual-Sport / Off-Road)
*   **Targeting Priority**: **Extremely High** (Primary target for Solara's flagship **TR500 TrailMaster** aluminum case system).
*   **Profile**: Hardcore, seasoned dual-sport and overlanding riders who tour off-grid. They value high-grade alloy panels, dual-sealing silicone waterproof gaskets, robust subframe mounts, crash bars, and radiator protectors.
*   **Commercial Alignment**: High-margin B2B wholesale replenishment and B2C premium adventure upgrades.

### D. The Daily Metro Commuter
*   **Liferay Taxonomy Category**: `"Micro-Mobility Rider"`
*   **Attitude Segment**: Urban (Commuter) / Electric
*   **Targeting Priority**: **High (High Volume Accessory Driver)**
*   **Profile**: Daily city riders navigating traffic on scooters, commuters, or lightweight electric motorbikes. They prioritize rain protection (AeroVent screens), compact lockable top cases (to stash full-face helmets), and vibration-dampening smartphone mounts with integrated Qi2 wireless charging.
*   **Commercial Alignment**: High-volume B2C accessory sales and rapid wholesale dealer stock turnover.

### E. The Heritage Classic Rider
*   **Liferay Taxonomy Category**: `"Retro Enthusiast"`
*   **Attitude Segment**: Café Racer (Classic)
*   **Targeting Priority**: **Medium (Niche Lifestyle Brand Builder)**
*   **Profile**: Owners of retro-styled, classic, cruiser, or customized scrambler bikes. They value aesthetic styling over high-tech utility, prioritizing leather saddlebags, retro canvas windshields, and classic polished-alloy mounting plates.
*   **Commercial Alignment**: Niche B2C specialty sales on styling gear.
